# OpenChess identity

Working product name follows the user's existing open-chess repository. Trademark, domain and store-name availability have not been checked; no claim of registration or availability is made.

Original identity: graphite surfaces, violet accent, warm walnut board, self-authored knight wordmark symbol and animal bot portraits. DM Sans is bundled under its open font license. Copy is calm, curious and direct; learning and exploration are more important than win/loss pressure. Chess-piece artwork is self-authored SVG.

This project reconstructs features and familiar board/review patterns from public documentation. It does not reproduce proprietary assets, bot characters, private endpoints or an exact visual identity. Licensed engine copyright notices are intentionally retained and excluded from application-brand checks.
