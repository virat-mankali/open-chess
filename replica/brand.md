# OpenChess identity

Working product name follows the user's existing open-chess repository. Trademark, domain and store-name availability have not been checked; no claim of registration or availability is made.

Revised at the user’s explicit request: warm charcoal surfaces, green controls and a green-and-cream board following the familiar Chess.com interface. Keep the OpenChess wordmark and original animal bot portraits. DM Sans is bundled under its open font license. Copy is calm, curious and direct; learning and exploration are more important than win/loss pressure. Pieces use the established Cburnett SVG set by Colin M. L. Burnett, with GPL-2.0-or-later source and attribution bundled.

This project reconstructs features and familiar board/review patterns from public documentation. It does not reproduce proprietary assets, bot characters, private endpoints or its wordmark. Licensed engine copyright notices are intentionally retained and excluded from application-brand checks.
