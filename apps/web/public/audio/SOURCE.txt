OpenChess edits: downmix to mono, trim to 120 ms, fade the last 40 ms and reduce
level. Capture combines two of these recorded contacts, 18 ms apart, at 75%
and 85% amplitude. No oscillators, pitch shifting, reverb or tonal cues.
Checks/review/retry use move.wav; captures/castles/promotions/finish use capture.wav.
The user's persisted volume is applied at playback, as before.

Reproduce with FFmpeg (tested with 7.1.5):
ffmpeg -i Move.ogg -af 'pan=mono|c0=0.5*c0+0.5*c1,atrim=duration=0.12,afade=t=out:st=0.08:d=0.04,volume=0.7' -ar 48000 -c:a pcm_s16le move.wav
ffmpeg -i move.wav -filter_complex '[0:a]asplit=2[first][second];[first]volume=0.75[a];[second]adelay=18,volume=0.85[b];[a][b]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0' -c:a pcm_s16le capture.wav
